Prepare for Interacting Futures

A short synopsis of M. Murali’s From Prediction to Possibility: AI, Quantum Computing and Dimensional Antifragility with an operating implication for enterprise leaders.

By NetworkGain Editorial

NetworkGain Point of View

A short synopsis of M. Murali’s From Prediction to Possibility: AI, Quantum Computing and Dimensional Antifragility with an operating implication for enterprise leaders.

Source: From Prediction to Possibility: AI, Quantum Computing and Dimensional Antifragility, by M. Murali, published 2026-07-08. This is an independently written synopsis; the operating implication is NetworkGain’s editorial interpretation.

M. Murali proposes extending foresight beyond a small set of forecasts towards exploring interacting disruptions. A supplier failure, energy constraint and demand shift can combine in ways that separate risk assessments miss. He calls the capacity to learn from such simulated combinations dimensional antifragility.

AI supplies candidate scenarios and interpretations in this proposed architecture. Quantum and quantum-inspired methods are presented as possible tools for selected mathematical problems, with algorithmic and engineering limitations acknowledged. The article offers a research direction rather than evidence of a general-purpose forecasting system.

The NetworkGain operating implication

choose two dependencies that could fail together and rehearse the combined event. Record which decisions, reserves and alternatives improve the response. Judge the exercise by useful changes made afterwards; a larger scenario catalogue alone does not establish readiness.
